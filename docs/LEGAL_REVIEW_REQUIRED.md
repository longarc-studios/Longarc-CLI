# Legal review gate

Publication of an official binary release is blocked until counsel or an authorized Long Arc decision-maker approves:

- the license for this public installer repository;
- the proprietary runtime notice and end-user terms;
- third-party notices and redistribution obligations;
- export, privacy, warranty, support, and reverse-engineering language;
- any patent or trade-secret implications of shipping the native Memory Lane runtime.

This file records a gate. It is not legal advice or a substitute for approved terms.
